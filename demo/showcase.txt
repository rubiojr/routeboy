# A tour of Routeboy, about 50 seconds long.
#
#   FADE_AUTOPILOT=demo/showcase.txt go run -tags autopilot .
#
# The script language is Fade's autopilot (docs/guide/15-scripted-demos.md
# in Fade). The names Routeboy gives its parts (map, bar, profile, track1…)
# are in autopilot.go. The tracks are the first two of the Tracks page, so any
# folder will do. Keep the mouse off the window while it runs.

# The map opens on Madrid.
wait idle
wait 2s

# Look around: drag the map, spread two fingers, double tap, then zoom out.
drag map at 0.65 0.55 by -150 70 over 1.2s
wait idle
wait 0.5s
pinch map 2 over 1.2s
wait idle
wait 0.8s
doubletap map at 0.4 0.45
wait idle
wait 0.8s
tap icon:list-remove
wait idle
tap icon:list-remove
wait idle
wait 1s

# Find a place and keep it.
tap search
wait 0.4s
type "Templo de Debod"
wait result1
wait 1.2s
tap "museum" in results
wait idle
wait 1.5s
tap icon:star-new
wait 1.5s

# The tracks: check two, then show the second on the map.
tap "Tracks"
wait track2
wait 1.2s
tap track1
wait 0.6s
tap track2
wait 1s
tap icon:mark-location in track2
wait idle
wait 1.5s

# The current track's details, and a walk along its elevation profile.
tap bar
wait profile
wait 1s
drag profile at 0.02 0.5 to 0.98 0.5 over 4s
wait 1s
drag profile at 0.98 0.5 to 0.6 0.5 over 1.5s
wait 1s
tap "Zoom to Track"
wait idle
wait 1.5s

# Back to the place kept.
tap "Bookmarks"
wait 1.5s
tap "Debod"
wait idle
wait 1.5s

# Places visited, and the About page.
tap "Places"
wait 1.5s
tap icon:open-menu
wait "About Routeboy"
wait 0.6s
tap "About Routeboy"
wait 2.5s
tap icon:go-previous
wait 0.6s
tap "Map"
wait idle
wait 2s
